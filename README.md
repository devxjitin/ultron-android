# Ultron — Your Personal Voice Assistant

Ultron is a general-purpose voice assistant. Anyone can just walk up, tap the mic, and
talk — it listens, understands, and answers back naturally, in real time. It isn't
built for one narrow job — it's designed to be your everyday assistant, wherever you need
one.

## What Ultron can do

- 🎙️ **Natural voice conversations** — just speak, no typing, no menus to navigate
- 🧠 **General-purpose intelligence** — ask it anything, from quick facts to detailed
  explanations
- 📚 **Learns what you teach it** — set up your own information and Ultron will use it
  when answering
- 🗣️ **Multilingual** — supports English and Hindi
- 👩 **Natural voices** — choose from warm, human-like voice options
- ⚙️ **Fully customizable** — personality, knowledge, and behavior can all be tuned
  without any rebuild
- 📱 **Always-on kiosk mode** — built for tablets, ready to greet anyone who walks up
- 🔒 **Privacy-conscious** — the short context used to resume a conversation is cleared when
  a visitor's turn ends (stop, walk away, or a manual "New conversation"), so it does not
  carry over to the next person. Chat history is stored on-device (up to 50 sessions, 200
  turns each) and can be viewed or deleted from the History screen; knowledge can be added or
  removed from the Knowledge page.

## Where Ultron fits in

Ultron isn't tied to any one industry. It works anywhere someone might need a helpful
voice:

- 🏢 **Offices** — greet visitors, answer common questions, direct people around
- 🏫 **Schools & institutions** — help with admissions, information, and everyday queries
- 🏥 **Clinics & healthcare** — guide visitors, share information, ease front-desk load
- 🛍️ **Retail & stores** — assist customers, answer product questions
- 🎉 **Events** — welcome guests, answer FAQs, keep things running smoothly
- 🏠 **Anywhere else** — Ultron adapts to whatever you set it up for

## Why Ultron

- **Talks like a person, not a machine** — real-time, natural back-and-forth conversation
- **No coding needed to customize** — update its knowledge and personality anytime
- **Built for real-world use** — designed to run reliably as an always-on kiosk
- **Yours to shape** — Ultron becomes whatever assistant your situation calls for
